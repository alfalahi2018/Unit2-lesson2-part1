Teacher plan · 25 minutes

Class: Grade 7 · Unit 2: Awesome animals · Lesson 2, part 1 (Class Book, Team Talk, page 43)
Lesson aim: to read and understand a dialogue about animals.
Target language: Watch out! Wow! It’s so cute!
Materials: Unit 2 flashcards and poster, Stop/Go cards, True/False response cards, a picture of a hedgehog, Photocopiable T2, device or projector.

TimeStageWhat happensTeacher’s Book link
0–4Warm-up
Stop 1: Word Check		Review the Lesson 1 vocabulary. Students choose the correct sentence for each picture. Use lollipop sticks to ask students to say a sentence.“Starting the lesson”: students say or write a sentence with each new word, using flashcards as prompts.
4–8Presentation
Stop 2: Expression Pop		Write the expressions on the board. Students drag Watch out! / Wow! / It’s so cute! to the right scene and say a sentence.“Presentation”: write the expressions, ask students to try and say a sentence with them.
8–14Formative 1
Stop 3: Read the Trail		Predict: indoors or outdoors? Listen and read the dialogue (audio 2.3). Find the lines that match the clues.Practice, Class Book 2.3: listen and read. Describe the animal they find.
14–19Formative 2
Stop 4: Stop or Go?		True/False with evidence. Use the Stop/Go cards in class too, then share answers.Stop/Go technique and True/False response cards; read the dialogue again and answer.
19–25Closing
Stop 5: Facts and a Fib		Find the fib about the hedgehog and other animals. Pairs tell a partner about an interesting animal they found. Exit ticket.“Finishing the lesson”: Three facts and a fib; Activity 4 discussion question.

Differentiation

  • Support 🌱: pictures and audio help in Word Check, hint words in Expression Pop, evidence shown after each Stop/Go, only two choices in Facts and a Fib.
  • Standard 🌿: full activities, evidence shown after each answer.
  • Challenge 🌳: no pictures in Word Check, students must choose the evidence line, and write about an interesting animal they found.

Notes and keys

  • The dialogue in the game is adapted from Class Book page 43. Check it against your book and edit the DL list if needed.
  • Where is it? Outdoors (a park). Mystery animal: a hedgehog (spines, small paws, whiskers).
  • Hedgehog note from the Teacher’s Book: a nocturnal mammal with a thick layer of spines; it rolls into a ball.
  • Deaf/hard of hearing students: the whole dialogue is written; audio is optional.
  • Extra: Photocopiable T2.
